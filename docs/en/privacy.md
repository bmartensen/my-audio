## 2. The My Audio app

My Audio works entirely on your device. It needs no account, contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting and transmits no data to the provider or to third parties. The Android app does not request the internet permission.

- **Recordings, imported audio files, projects and notes** are stored locally: on Android in the app's private storage, on Windows in *Documents/My Audio*.
- **Sharing and backups** only happen when you start them. The file goes to the app or location you choose. That provider's terms apply to its processing.
- **Android backup and transfer:** if you have set up a device backup in Android's settings, Android may include your projects and effect library in that backup, and a transfer to a new phone takes them along. Calibration and device settings stay on the phone. You control this in the system settings. Your backup provider's terms apply (usually Google).

**Android permissions**

- **Microphone:** for recording, calibration, the tuner and live monitoring. The microphone is only open at those times.
- **Notifications and foreground service:** so playback and recording continue in the background and with the screen off, with a visible notification.
- **Modify audio settings:** to use the selected playback device and microphone, such as a USB interface or a Bluetooth headset.

Links in **About My Audio** (website, privacy, e-mail) open your browser or e-mail app. Sections 3 and 5 apply.

No automated decision-making or profiling (Art. 22 GDPR) takes place.
